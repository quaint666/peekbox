# English

**Seasonal banner (set under Campaigns):** Tết 2027: pick your hamper and delivery date early for the best choice

**Small pill above the headline:** Tết · 20/11 · 8/3

**Headline:** A gift hamper where you see every item inside

**Description:** Set a budget, pick items, and see each item's brand, weight, origin and expiry date. The system adds up the total and checks every expiry date against your delivery date.

**Buttons:** Build your hamper | See our promise

## Three promises
1. **See every item.** Every item shows its brand, weight, origin and expiry date. The cart, the order and the email list them all.
2. **Budget in hand.** You set the amount first. The budget bar shows what is left, so you never add things up yourself.
3. **Safe expiry dates.** An item that expires before the delivery date (plus a safety margin) cannot be ordered.

## How it works
1. Set a budget: Enter an amount and a delivery date.
2. Pick items: Read each item's details, then add it to the box.
3. Choose packaging: Paper box, hard box or rattan basket.
4. Place the order: Pay with ZaloPay or on delivery.

## For every occasion
- **Tết:** Gift hampers for family, partners and colleagues at the start of the year.
- **20/11:** Thank-you hampers for teachers, compact and easy to split across a class budget.
- **8/3:** Hampers for mothers, wives, girlfriends and women colleagues.
