Interaction states (dark)

Every control has these five looks. Hover changes only apply where a mouse exists (@media (hover: hover)); a phone shows pressed, never hover. Colour changes take 150 ms and movement 200 ms; with “reduce motion” on, movement is dropped and only the colour change remains. The focus ring is never removed.

Item card (builder)
The photo and the name are links to the item page; “Thêm” is its own button. Hover anywhere on the card: it lifts 2 px with a deeper shadow and a primary border, the photo zooms to 105 % and the name turns primary and underlined (reduce motion: no lift, no zoom). Focus rings the link that has it. In the box, “Thêm” becomes − n +. A sold-out card is greyed and does not react.
